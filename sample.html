Invitation · In your own words

Formation Plan

One four-week experiment.

Sabbath · Establishing

Protect a three-hour Sabbath block on the same day each week. Decide the start and finish time, what work will stop, one form of delight and one simple way you will turn your attention toward God.

Week 1 — Set the conditions. Put the block in the calendar and decide in advance what work and unnecessary screens will stop.
Week 2 — Practise and notice. Repeat the block. Afterwards, write one sentence about what became easier, harder or more visible in you.
Week 3 — Draw from your Anchor (Community). Tell one trusted person what you are practising and consider sharing part of Sabbath — a meal, walk or worship — without turning it into another obligation.
Week 4 — Discern the fruit. Name what helped you become more present to God and more like Jesus, what felt forced, and the one element worth carrying forward.
