Stage 7 · Formation Plan

One four-week experiment.

Protect one repeatable block each week to stop ordinary productivity and make room for rest, worship and delight.

Week 1 — Make space. Choose a realistic recurring block and decide in advance what you will stop.
Week 2 — Receive. Begin with something non-productive that helps you receive God’s presence.
Week 3 — Draw from your Anchor. Let Community support Sabbath.
Week 4 — Discern. Notice what resistance appeared and what became life-giving.
