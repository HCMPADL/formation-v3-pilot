# Formation V4.1 Adaptive Calibration Status

## Status

**Calibration required.**

The V4.1 item bank materially changes the constructs represented by the assessment. Every domain now deliberately maps to:

**Practice → Posture → Integration → Fruit**

Deeper Discernment v1.2 also changes the adaptive evidence structure so Practice/Posture is interpreted in relation to Integration/Fruit.

For that reason, the earlier synthetic parallel-validation results for adaptive v1.1 are historical engineering evidence only. They must not be quoted as accuracy or agreement estimates for the V4.1 assessment.

## V4.1 adaptive structure

Universal foundation: 84 items.

For every domain this includes:

- Practice 1
- Posture 1
- Integration 1
- Fruit 1
- Integration 2
- Fruit 2

The second Practice and Posture items are reserved and released when greater evidence could materially change Attention, Anchor, threshold interpretation, or the relationship between Practice/Posture and Integration/Fruit.

Maximum remains 112 items.

## Required next validation

Before treating V4.1 adaptive results as calibrated, collect a cohort with the complete 112-item response set and score each participant twice:

1. complete 112-item V4.1 result;
2. simulated adaptive v1.2 stop-point result.

Primary engineering targets:

- ≥95% exact Attention agreement where a differentiated Attention is named;
- ≥95% Anchor agreement;
- ≥95% domain-pattern agreement;
- no concerning systematic differences related to contextual constraints;
- acceptable stability of qualitative Christlike Fruit themes;
- acceptable participant-rated resonance and usefulness.

These remain engineering and instrument-development targets. They do not by themselves establish clinical or psychometric validation of spiritual maturity, and Formation should never make that claim.
