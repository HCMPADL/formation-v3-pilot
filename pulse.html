What the questions do

This isn't a scorecard.

Your answers build a picture across six parts of life. LIFEMAP notices where there is strength, where there is strain, and what may need care.

Your circumstances are then added because the same answer can mean something different in a different season.

What LIFEMAP is listening for

Five possible directions

The pattern in your answers may point toward one of five directions. They aren't scores or personality types. Think of them as simple ways of naming what this season may be asking of you.

Direction 1 of 5

RESTORE

Recover and receive when life is depleted.

When energy, rest or capacity are running thin, the invitation may be to stop asking more of yourself and make room for recovery.

Direction 2 of 5

RECONNECT

Strengthen connection with God and people.

When life is moving but relationship feels distant, the next step may be presence, honesty and companionship rather than more activity.

Direction 3 of 5

REORDER

Create room when life has become crowded.

When pressure, pace or commitments are taking too much space, the invitation may be to simplify, set a boundary or let something go.

Direction 4 of 5

RENEW

Gently strengthen an area that has become thin.

When life is not depleted but something has faded, a small life-giving rhythm may help restore attention, energy or desire.

Direction 5 of 5

ABIDE

Receive and remain in what is already giving life.

Not every season needs fixing. When there is life, connection and steadiness, the invitation may simply be gratitude, presence and remaining with God in it.
