Before you begin

Answer for your real life.

Formation is not asking how spiritually mature you think you should be. Respond from what is actually true in this season — what your ordinary life currently looks and feels like.

How to use the scale

Choose the response that best describes your life now.

Not true
This is generally not part of my life or experience at present.
Slightly true
There are occasional signs of this, but it is not yet a meaningful or consistent part of my life.
Somewhat true
This is present to some degree, although it varies or is still taking shape.
Mostly true
This is regularly true and has a meaningful place in my ordinary life.
Very true
This is deeply characteristic of my life in this season.
Not applicable
Use this when a statement genuinely does not apply to your circumstances or you are unable to answer it.

A helpful rule: don't answer from your best week, your worst week, or the person you hope to become. Think about the pattern of your life over this current season.
