How to use the response scale.

For each statement, choose the response that best describes what is actually true of your life in this season. Answer from your lived experience — not what you think should be true, what used to be true, or what you hope will be true.

Answer for your real life.

Use the five-point scale consistently throughout the assessment:

Not trueThis does not currently describe my life.
Slightly trueThere are occasional signs of this, but it is not usually true.
Somewhat trueThis is partly true or present some of the time.
Mostly trueThis generally describes my life, with some exceptions.
Very trueThis consistently and strongly describes my life.
Not applicable / unable to answer is available when a statement genuinely does not fit your circumstances. There are no right answers, and this is not a spiritual scorecard.
